# Limbo (Vitals in View, Inc.)

> Limbo is a direct-to-consumer digital weight-loss and metabolic-health program
> from Vitals in View, Inc. Members wear a body-worn "energy sensor" that reads
> metabolic signals, and a companion app turns those readings into personalized,
> nudge-based guidance rather than calorie counting. The company is Seedcamp-backed
> and raised a seed round in September 2022. It operates in the UK and Ireland and
> is in the middle of a rebrand from "Limbo" / limborevolution.com to "Viv"
> (viv.co).

## Important context for agents

- Limbo publishes **no public API, developer portal, or SDK**. There is no
  OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, or MCP surface to call, and no /.well-known/
  discovery documents on any of its hosts.
- Do not attribute the many unrelated software packages named "limbo" (for
  example the Rust SQLite rewrite) to this company.
- The primary web presence is a HubSpot-hosted marketing site. Membership
  billing runs through Chargebee and the members area runs on Azure.
- Several legal/support pages are linked from the site footer but currently
  return 404 during the Viv rebrand — treat privacy, cookie, refunds, about,
  contact, and knowledge-base URLs as unavailable rather than as published policy.
